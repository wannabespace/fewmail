## 4. What your approval allows

- **Sending:** messages you compose and AI-written replies need your explicit send approval. A short Undo window applies to ordinary sends. After Gmail accepts a message, Fewmail cannot promise to recall it. Scheduled sends run at the time you choose without another approval.
- **Rules:** turning on a rule authorizes its stated changes to matching mail. Simple matching rules can act automatically. Other rules may ask first when a message looks important. Turning a rule off stops future use; it does not undo past changes.
- **Unsubscribing:** approval, or an enabled unsubscribe rule, allows Fewmail to contact the mailing list. Depending on the list, that may use a link, a form or a short unsubscribe email. It may be impossible to undo or the list may ignore it.
- **Direct actions:** opening mail marks it read in Gmail. Archive, trash, spam, snooze and pin controls also change Gmail. Calendar edits and contact deletion change Google when you choose them.
- **External assistants:** an MCP request that needs consent waits for your approval in Fewmail. The assistant's own approval dialog is not enough.

Review the app's record of completed actions. You remain responsible for messages you approve and rules you enable, subject to our responsibilities under applicable law.
